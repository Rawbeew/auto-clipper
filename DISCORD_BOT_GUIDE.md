# 💬 How to Receive Rendered Videos Directly on Discord

You can receive finished **9:16 vertical MP4 short video files** directly in any Discord server channel, complete with **CTR Titles**, **Description Captions**, and **SEO Hashtags**!

---

## 🛠️ 30-Second Discord Webhook Setup

1. Open your Discord server on desktop or mobile.
2. Right-click the channel where you want videos delivered -> **Edit Channel**.
3. Go to **Integrations** -> Click **Webhooks** -> **New Webhook**.
4. Give your webhook a name (e.g. `AutoClipper Bot`).
5. Click **Copy Webhook URL**. The URL will look like:
   ```text
   https://discord.com/api/webhooks/123456789/AbCdEfGhIjKlMnOpQrStUvWxYz
   ```
6. Open **[github.com/Rawbeew/auto-clipper/settings/secrets/actions](https://github.com/Rawbeew/auto-clipper/settings/secrets/actions)**.
7. Click **New repository secret**:
   - **Name**: `DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL`
   - **Value**: Paste your Discord Webhook URL.

---

## 📦 What Your Discord Channel Receives

Whenever you trigger a video creation request, your Discord channel receives:

1. 📹 **High-Definition 1080x1920 MP4 Video File Attached** (Directly streamable inside Discord).

   `Why You Forget 90% of Your Dreams in 5 Minutes`

   `Have you ever woken up remembering an intense dream, only for it to vanish 5 minutes later? Here is the scientific explanation!`
4. 🏷️ **SEO Hashtags**:
   `#Shorts #Dreams #BrainSecrets #FYP #Viral #DidYouKnow`
5. 🔥 **Virality Score**: `98/100`

Now you can review the video on your phone or desktop, copy the title and tags with 1 click, and upload whenever you're ready!
