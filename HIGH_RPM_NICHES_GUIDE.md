## 💡 Why These Niches Earn Up to 50x More Per View

1. **Advertiser High Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**:
   - Banks, tax software, legal services, SaaS platforms, and brokers pay $20–$100+ per lead. They bid aggressively for ad slots on finance, tax, and SaaS channels.
2. **Mid-Roll Ad Frequency on Long-Form Content**:
   - Creating 15 to 35-minute videos in these niches allows placing 3 to 6 mid-roll ads per video, raising your effective RPM from $2/1,000 views up to **$20+/1,000 views**.
